![N8N Workflow Overview](/assets/images/n8n_workflow.jpg)

This workflow implements an AI-powered automation system that enhances the efficiency of customer support ticket management in Zendesk.  
Developed using n8n and Claude AI, it analyzes incoming tickets, classifies them by urgency and sentiment, and performs automated actions across connected platforms.

The system integrates Zendesk, Google Sheets, Jira, and Slack to streamline the entire ticket lifecycle.  
New tickets are captured through a webhook, processed for content and tone, and routed accordingly.  
Critical tickets trigger automated Jira issue creation and Slack notifications, while standard tickets are logged in Google Sheets and updated in Zendesk with relevant responses.

This project demonstrates advanced capabilities in AI integration, workflow automation, and multi-platform orchestration, providing a scalable and reliable foundation for intelligent support operations.
